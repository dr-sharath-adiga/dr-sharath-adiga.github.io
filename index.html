Consultant Neurologist in Udupi

Dr. Sharath Adiga M

MBBS, MD (General Medicine), DM (Neurology)

Dr. Sharath Adiga is a consultant neurologist trained at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru. He sees patients at Hitech Medicare Hospital, Udupi.

He evaluates and treats disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves and muscles, including stroke, epilepsy, headache and migraine, Parkinson’s disease, neuropathies and neuroimmunological conditions.

Conditions treated

Specialist evaluation and long-term care across the full range of neurological disorders.

  • Stroke and cerebrovascular disordersIschemic stroke and brain hemorrhage, Transient ischemic attack (TIA)
  • Epilepsy and seizure disordersFirst-time seizures, Epilepsy and recurrent seizures
  • Headache and migraineMigraine, Tension-type headache
  • Movement disordersParkinson’s disease and parkinsonism, Essential tremor and other tremor disorders
  • Neuropathy, nerve and muscle disordersPeripheral neuropathy, Diabetic neuropathy
  • Neuroimmunological and demyelinating disordersMultiple sclerosis (MS), Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder (NMOSD)
  • Sleep disordersInsomnia and sleep disturbances, Excessive daytime sleepiness
  • Memory and cognitive disordersAlzheimer’s disease, Dementia and other cognitive disorders
  • Vertigo, dizziness and balance disordersVertigo and dizziness, Vestibular disorders
  • Spine and spinal cord disordersCervical and lumbar radiculopathy, Cervical and lumbar spondylosis with neurological symptoms
  • Neurological infectionsMeningitis and encephalitis, Neurotuberculosis
  • Other neurological conditionsFacial palsy and cranial nerve disorders, Trigeminal neuralgia and facial pain

Tests and procedures

Neurological tests help confirm a diagnosis and guide treatment. Dr. Adiga is trained in:

  • EEG and Video EEG
  • Nerve conduction studies and EMG
  • Evoked potentials (VEP, BERA)
  • Thrombolysis for acute stroke
  • Botulinum toxin injections
