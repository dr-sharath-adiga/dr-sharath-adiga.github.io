Tests and procedures

Dr. Sharath Adiga has clinical training and experience in the following neurological investigations and procedures.

  • EEG and Video EEGRecords the brain’s electrical activity. Used to diagnose and classify epilepsy and seizures.
  • Nerve conduction studies (NCS)Measures how well nerves carry signals. Used for neuropathy, nerve injury and carpal tunnel syndrome.
  • Electromyography (EMG)Tests the electrical activity of muscles to tell nerve problems from muscle problems.
  • Repetitive nerve stimulation (RNS)Checks the nerve–muscle junction. Used mainly in the diagnosis of myasthenia gravis.
  • Visual evoked potentials (VEP)Measures the brain’s response to visual signals. Helpful in optic neuritis and multiple sclerosis.
  • Brainstem evoked response audiometry (BERA)Tests the hearing pathway from the ear through the brainstem.
  • Intravenous thrombolysisClot-dissolving treatment for eligible patients with acute ischemic stroke, given within the time window.
  • Botulinum toxin injectionsFor selected conditions, including dystonia, hemifacial spasm and blepharospasm.
  • Nerve and muscle biopsyTissue sampling to help diagnose selected nerve and muscle diseases.

Whether a test is needed is decided after a consultation. Availability of specific tests may vary; please confirm when booking.
